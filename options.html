      What this PIN does and does not do. It stops the settings being changed casually. It cannot stop someone who knows Chrome well: any user can disable Guardian from chrome://extensions, and that is true of every extension. For protection that cannot be switched off, install Guardian through Chrome Enterprise policy (ExtensionInstallForcelist, with ExtensionSettings blocking removal).

Protection

Guardian checks each page as it loads and blocks what looks unsafe.

Scan pages automatically
Turn this off and nothing is checked.
Block flagged pages
Covers the page with a warning. Off means warn via the icon only.
Keyword safety net
Blocks when the page text plainly says what it is, even if the picture looks ordinary. Catches search results for explicit terms.
Double-check before blocking
Takes a second look at anything flagged or borderline, and uses the more cautious of the two results.
Re-check videos every
A playing video never reloads the page, so it is checked on a timer.

        Security

        The PIN protects these settings, and unlocking a blocked page.

        What this PIN does and does not do. It stops the settings being changed casually, and it is required to reveal a blocked page. It cannot stop someone who knows Chrome well: any user can disable Guardian from chrome://extensions, and that is true of every extension. For protection that cannot be switched off, install Guardian through Chrome Enterprise policy (ExtensionInstallForcelist, with ExtensionSettings blocking removal).

        Reset Guardian

        Erases the PIN, both site lists, the activity history and every setting. Guardian returns to its first-run state. This cannot be undone.
